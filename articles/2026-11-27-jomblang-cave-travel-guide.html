Jomblang Cave: Light Beams & Underground Wonder

Some places you visit. Jomblang is a place you descend into — 60 metres down on a rope, harness on, into a hidden world beneath the jungle of Gunungkidul.

At the bottom, a short walk through an ancient forest inside the cave leads to the famous heavenly light: a beam of sunlight cutting through the dark, landing exactly where you stand. Around midday it is at its best, and for a few minutes the whole cave glows.

The descent is run by a trained cave team, one person at a time, and the cave floor can be muddy — wear clothes you don't mind getting dirty and shoes with grip. Bring a spare dry set for after.

  • Go around midday for the strongest light beam.
  • Wear a helmet and harness — provided by the cave team.
  • Bring a waterproof bag for your phone and camera.
