Included & Non-Included

Jogja tours: entrance tickets are not included. East Java overland: everything is included, up to drop-off in Jogja or Ketapang Harbour.

✔ Included · Jogja Tours

  • Air-conditioned car & experienced driver
  • Fuel, parking and tolls
  • Pick-up & drop-off at your hotel, homestay or station in Jogja
  • Mineral water

✖ Not Included · Jogja Tours

  • Entrance tickets (Borobudur, Prambanan, Punthuk Setumbu, Jomblang, Pindul and others)
  • Merapi Jeep: 4x4 jeep rental and area ticket
  • Meals & drinks
  • Personal expenses & tips
  • Accommodation & personal travel insurance

✔ Included · East Java Overland (All Inclusive)

  • Vehicle & driver from Jogja, fuel, tolls and parking
  • All entrance tickets (Bromo, Ijen Crater, Tumpak Sewu)
  • Bromo jeep
  • Accommodation and meals as per itinerary
  • Guide and equipment (including Ijen gas mask)
  • Pick-up in Yogyakarta
  • Drop-off in Yogyakarta or near Ketapang Harbour, Banyuwangi

✖ Not Included · East Java Overland

  • Personal expenses (souvenirs, snacks)
  • Meals & drinks outside the itinerary
  • Tips for driver & guide (optional)
  • Personal travel insurance

Jogja tour prices are per person and get cheaper for larger groups (1, 2, 3–5, and 6 or more guests). Climbing Borobudur temple is closed every Monday for maintenance. The Merapi jeep route may change depending on weather and volcano status.

Frequently Asked Questions

 

What is the difference between Sharing and Private?

Sharing means you join other guests; Private is only for your own group. Both are priced per person and get cheaper for larger groups (1, 2, 3–5, and 6 or more guests). Bromo & Ijen is priced per person with a minimum of 2 guests.

What is included in the price?

Jogja tours: car, driver, fuel, parking and tolls are included. Entrance tickets are not included and are paid separately. East Java overland (Bromo & Ijen): everything is included, including tickets, Bromo jeep, accommodation, meals, guide, and drop-off in Yogyakarta or near Ketapang Harbour.

What time is pick-up?

Sunrise tours 03:30, regular temple tour 07:00–09:00, Jomblang & Pindul 07:00, Merapi Jeep 07:00 or 13:00. Bromo & Ijen follows the tour schedule.

How does the Bromo, Tumpak Sewu & Ijen trip work?

You are picked up in Yogyakarta and driven to East Java. After Ijen, you can be taken back to Jogja or dropped off near Ketapang Harbour to continue to Bali.
