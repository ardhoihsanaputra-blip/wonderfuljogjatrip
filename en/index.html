Included & Excluded

Yogyakarta day tours: attraction entrance tickets are excluded. East Java overland tours: all-inclusive until drop-off in Yogyakarta or Ketapang Port (for Bali).

✔ Included · Yogyakarta Day Tours

  • Air-conditioned private/shared car & English-speaking driver
  • Fuel, parking fees, and highway tolls
  • Pickup & drop-off at hotels, homestays, or train stations in Yogyakarta
  • Bottled mineral water

✖ Excluded · Yogyakarta Day Tours

  • Attraction entrance tickets (Borobudur, Prambanan, Punthuk Setumbu, Jomblang Cave, Pindul Cave, etc.)
  • Merapi Jeep Tour: 4x4 Jeep rental and volcano area entry fee
  • Meals & drinks
  • Personal expenses & tips
  • Accommodation & personal travel insurance

✔ Included · East Java Overland (All-Inclusive)

  • Vehicle & driver from Yogyakarta, fuel, tolls, and parking
  • All entrance tickets (Mount Bromo, Ijen Crater, Tumpak Sewu Waterfall)
  • 4x4 Jeep at Mount Bromo
  • Accommodation and meals as per itinerary
  • Local trekking guide & safety gear (including Ijen gas mask)
  • Direct pickup in Yogyakarta
  • Drop-off in Yogyakarta or Ketapang Ferry Port, Banyuwangi (to Bali)

✖ Excluded · East Java Overland

  • Personal expenses (souvenirs, snacks)
  • Meals & beverages outside the itinerary
  • Voluntary tips for driver & local guides
  • Personal travel insurance

Yogyakarta tour rates are priced per person and decrease for larger groups (1, 2, 3–5, and 6+ participants). Climbing up Borobudur Temple structure is closed every Monday. Merapi 4x4 Jeep routes may adjust depending on weather and volcanic activity.

Frequently Asked Questions

 

What is the difference between Sharing and Private tours?

Sharing tours group you with fellow travelers on a fixed schedule for budget-friendly rates. Private tours reserve the vehicle exclusively for your party with flexible pacing. Both are priced per person with tiered discounts for larger groups (1, 2, 3–5, and 6+ guests). Bromo & Ijen overland packages require a minimum of 2 participants.

What is included in the tour price?

For Yogyakarta day tours, the price covers the air-conditioned vehicle, driver, fuel, parking, and tolls—attraction entrance tickets are paid separately on-site. For East Java overland tours (Bromo & Ijen), everything is included: transport, entrance tickets, Bromo 4x4 Jeep, accommodation, meals, local guides, and drop-off in Yogyakarta or Ketapang Ferry Port.

What are the pickup times?

Sunrise tours start at 03:30 AM, regular temple tours at 07:00–09:00 AM, Jomblang & Pindul Cave at 07:00 AM, and Merapi Jeep at 07:00 AM or 01:00 PM. East Java overland trips depart according to the multi-day schedule.

How do the Bromo, Tumpak Sewu & Ijen tours from Jogja work?

You will be picked up directly from your accommodation in Yogyakarta and driven overland to East Java. After completing the Ijen Crater trek, you can choose either return transfer to Yogyakarta or drop-off at Ketapang Ferry Port in Banyuwangi to continue seamlessly to Bali.
