    Know Before You Go
    • Prambanan Temple Monday Access: At Prambanan Temple, climbing up into the main inner temple zone occasionally has Monday restrictions for routine maintenance, while the surrounding park grounds remain open. Borobudur temple climbs have limited daily quotas and use Upanat sandals provided on-site.
    • Mount Merapi Weather & Volcanic Safety: Merapi 4x4 Jeep trails and viewpoints may be adjusted or rerouted at short notice depending on weather conditions and official volcanic activity advisories.
    • Cave & Mountain Gear: Bring a change of clothes and water shoes/sandals for Pindul Cave tubing, sturdy closed-toe footwear for Jomblang Cave and temple stairs, and a warm windproof jacket for sunrise viewpoints (Punthuk Setumbu, Bromo & Ijen).
    • H-1 Driver Coordination: Our team or assigned driver will contact you via WhatsApp the evening before your trip to reconfirm exact pickup time and hotel lobby location.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Are attraction entrance tickets included in Yogyakarta day tours?

    No. For all Yogyakarta day tours (Temples, Caves, and Merapi Jeep), attraction entrance tickets and 4x4 Jeep rental are excluded and paid separately on-site. However, every participant gets a free beer!

    What type of hotel is included in the East Java Overland packages?

    All East Java Overland tours (Bromo, Tumpak Sewu & Ijen) include 2-star or 3-star hotel accommodation with a sharing room arrangement of 2 guests per room, plus a free beer for every participant.

    What is the difference between Sharing and Private tours?

    Sharing tours group you with fellow travelers on a fixed schedule for budget-friendly rates. Private tours reserve the vehicle exclusively for your party with flexible pacing. East Java overland packages require a minimum of 2 participants.

    What are the pickup times?

    Borobudur sunrise tours start at 03:30 AM, Punthuk Setumbu sunrise at 04:00 AM, regular temple tours at 07:00–09:00 AM, Jomblang & Pindul Cave at 07:00 AM, and Merapi Jeep at 07:00 AM or 01:00 PM. East Java overland trips depart according to the multi-day schedule.
